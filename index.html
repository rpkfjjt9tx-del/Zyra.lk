Jewellery Care

A little care helps preserve the beauty of your Zyra pieces.

Keep your pieces looking their best

• Avoid contact with perfumes, lotions, cosmetics and harsh chemicals.

• Keep jewellery away from water, swimming pools and excessive moisture.

• Remove jewellery before showering, exercising, sleeping or doing household chores.

• Do not use Vim, scouring powders, dishwashing liquid, laundry detergents, bleach or surface cleaners to clean your jewellery. These products can affect the plating and finish.

• After wearing, gently wipe with a clean, soft, dry cloth.

• Store each piece separately in a dry place to reduce scratches and tangling.
