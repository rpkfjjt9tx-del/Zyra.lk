Sales & Profit Report

Non-pending and non-cancelled orders. Delivery fees are excluded. Profit = selling price − cost price. For older order items without a saved cost snapshot, the latest product cost is used as an estimate.

The date range applies to order sales, product costs, courier costs and business expenses. Orders and business expenses are loaded in pages so the report includes all records returned by the database. Net profit is marked PARTIAL when any sold item has no saved cost price. Enter missing costs before treating the figure as final.
