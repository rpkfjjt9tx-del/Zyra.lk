The Zyra Care Guide

Small habits make a difference. Give your pieces a little care after every wear.

Keep your jewellery beautiful

Keep it dryRemove jewellery before showering, swimming, exercising or washing up. Avoid prolonged moisture.
Perfume comes firstApply perfume, lotion and cosmetics before putting on your jewellery. Let them dry first.
Clean gentlyWipe with a clean, soft, dry cloth after wearing. Avoid abrasive cloths and harsh cleaners.
Store separatelyKeep each piece in its pouch or a dry jewellery box to reduce scratches and tangling.
Avoid chemicalsDo not use Vim, scouring powders, dishwashing liquid, laundry detergents, bleach or surface cleaners.
Handle with carePut jewellery on last and remove it first. Avoid pulling chains or bending delicate components.

Everyday care, timeless details. Thank you for choosing Zyra.lk.
