Sales & Profit Report

Non-pending and non-cancelled orders. Delivery fees are excluded. Profit = selling price − cost price. For older order items without a saved cost snapshot, the latest product cost is used as an estimate.

The date range applies to order sales, product costs, courier costs and business expenses. Order-derived figures are based on the latest 300 orders loaded; up to 2,000 expense records are loaded. Net profit uses recorded product and courier costs, so missing cost records can make it appear higher than the actual profit.
